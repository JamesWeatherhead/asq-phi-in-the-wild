## Evidence interpretation

All three use actual benchmark data rather than merely citing ASQ-PHI. Bao uses 250 queries; MedDeID and AegisVox use 1,051. AegisVox uses text queries to test its detector; its audio experiments use other corpora. Do not call this an ASQ speech dataset use. There is no verified product deployment or regulatory certification here.

The metrics are incompatible across studies. Bao measures label-agnostic overlap detection on replaced spans. MedDeID measures annotation-character recall and character over-redaction, with a separately labeled age sensitivity. AegisVox reports exact typed and boundary-tolerant entity metrics under both a reduced contract intersection and full ASQ ontology. A single leaderboard would be misleading.
