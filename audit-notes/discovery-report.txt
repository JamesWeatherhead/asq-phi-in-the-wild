## Verified commercial evaluation: Protostar AI
Primary source https://protostarai.com/validation.html . Entire 70-line rendered page and raw HTML reviewed. Page explicitly says last updated July 18, 2026. This is a page-update date, not independently proven first appearance of ASQ-PHI use. Class: evaluation / commercial self-report. Independent external group relative to dataset authors, but results are vendor-authored and were not rerun here. Suitable for verified-public-use count because named corpus and metrics are explicit.

ASQ-PHI is named alongside TAB in structured-identifier tests. The page reports clinical precision 0.98, recall 0.99, F1 0.98, and attributes July changes to annotation-policy corrections involving years, eponyms, drugs, and ages. It also gives overall ASQ-PHI recall 0.93 in another passage, so these results should not be combined or represented as one unqualified score. Evaluation uses entity-level overlap matching; category policies differ. No public executable benchmark artifacts or predictions are linked on the reviewed page. Its architectural guarantees are separate vendor claims, not established by ASQ-PHI testing. Avoid repeating the page's claim that all ages are outside Safe Harbor: ages over 89 require special treatment.
